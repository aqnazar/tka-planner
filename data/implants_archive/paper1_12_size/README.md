# Paper 1 implant library: 12 sizes (archive)

The 12-size library (S1-S4, M1-M4, L1-L4) that Paper 1's pipeline cut and fitted: the
femoral component (left and right), tibial plate, tibial insert and the femoral and
tibial cutting blocks with their shells, one folder per size, as exported from the
Fusion design of that time. `SizeChart.csv` is the chart that went with it
(femoral and tibial ML and AP, mm). `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's SHA-256, so the
library can be cited by hash.

This is an archive. It is the family the paper's E12 experiment reruns, and it is kept
unchanged. The newer catalogue, derived from the latest parametric Fusion models, is in
`data/implants/`, and the next family (wider size range, more sizes) will replace it as
the working library, with this folder and the present catalogue kept as archives.

Left out on purpose: the per-size `FD1Left_*` and `TD1Left_*` bone models in the original
export (331 MB). They are bones, not implants.
