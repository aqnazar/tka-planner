A knee implant made from the patient's own CT

A total knee replacement is normally chosen from a catalogue of sizes. Here the femoral component, tibial tray and insert are designed for each knee: a planner places the cuts on the bones from the CT, rules turn the cuts into the parameters of three Fusion designs, and Fusion rebuilds the parts. The parts are then reduced on the cut bones and flexed.

  1. Anatomy the segmented bones and the landmarks found on them
  2. Plan alignment and the five femoral and one tibial cut, live
  3. Implant the Fusion parameters, derived from the cuts by rules
  4. Reduce the built parts on the cut bones, flexed through 120°
  5. Build Fusion rebuilds the three parts from a changed plan (about a minute)

Research prototype, Satbayev University. Not a medical device. Knees are shown by case id only.
